Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about optimizing your resume for ATS filters.

A resume checker scans your resume to see how well it meets the standards used by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and recruiters. It looks at key factors such as keyword usage, formatting, section organization, and overall clarity by comparing your document to common job descriptions or ATS requirements to identify missing tokens or confusing layouts.

A good ATS score is 80% or higher. Scoring between 80–89% indicates a strong profile alignment that meets most criteria with only minor vocabulary gaps, while scores above 90% show exceptional match characteristics highly likely to clear automated parsing rules seamlessly.

Common critical compliance issues include complex structural formatting (such as dense tables, multi-column configurations, embedded images, or graphics), non-standard heading names that confuse system models, complete lack of technical job description keywords, and inconsistent date formatting parameters.

To enhance your score, explicitly incorporate missing technical skills and qualifications naturally into previous roles. Use standard section headers like "Work Experience" or "Skills," opt for single-column layouts without graphics or tables, spell out acronyms completely alongside their short forms, and thoroughly quantify achievements with measurable numeric data.
